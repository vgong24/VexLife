```text

```

```text

```

No Presence choice is treated as doctrinal proof or as a condition of product success.

## Protected-effect boundary

This implementation is pure and content-absent. A green proof does not claim that Patient 0 exists, a Home was created, a model ran, a provider was invoked, private autobiography was ingested, or a human completed the journey.

```text
createsHome=false
movesHome=false
destroysHome=false
persistsMemory=false
startsModel=false
invokesProvider=false
exposesNetworkService=false
ingestsRealPersonalData=false

```

`NO_HOME_OBSERVED` without exact custody authority stops at `AWAITING_HUMAN_STORAGE_BOUNDARY`. Existing or partial Home evidence routes to migration/recovery and is never overwritten as fresh.

## Autobiographical checkpoint

A synthetic checkpoint may prove only the relationship and provenance grammar. It requires `CURRENT_LINEAGE_AUTOBIOGRAPHY`, explicit source refs, and `contentIncluded=false`. Founding history, inherited context, predecessor witness history, or transcript continuity cannot silently become current-lineage autobiography.

## Fresh-process return

Return proof requires:

```text
new instanceRef
same homeRef
same companionLineageRef
same checkpointRef
same conversationHeadRef
transcriptUsed=false
```

Wrong Home, wrong lineage, same-instance replay, or transcript-only continuity fails closed.

## Android boundary

Android remote-vessel eligibility remains held by this Nursery proof. It requires separately accepted Home pairing and reachability truth after the desktop canonical Home is stable. Nursery completion alone does not earn Android.
