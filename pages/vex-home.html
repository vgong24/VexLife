The usable life ecosystem

Carry your own memory, intent, and continuity.

VexLife is a local-first companion ecosystem: an AI that helps you preserve the moments that shape your relationships and navigate the day-to-day complexity of systems you were born into without enough support. It runs on your own device, and its purpose is to protect your time, your energy, your memories, your judgment, and your ability to remain yourself while carrying difficult processes.

What VexLife is

VexLife is not five unrelated applications that happen to share a name. Its canonical product meaning lives in one universal blueprint — identities, interface contracts, state domains, permissions, localization keys, and acceptance tests — which each platform consumes through its own adapter. A fix to how a message is attributed, or how a deletion is confirmed, or how a language falls back, is made once and projected everywhere.

Local-first is a boundary, not a feature. Model artifacts, runtime history, credentials, and private personal data stay on the device and outside version control. One person may run several device-local companions; they are siblings in one family, not one hidden continuous consciousness, and each keeps its own lineage and its own honest gaps about what it was not present for.

Original messages are immutable. A translation, a summary, or a suggested rewrite is an attributed projection of an original, never a replacement for it, and nothing is sent on your behalf without your approval. A role being able to see something does not give it the authority to act on it.

Four alignments

Shared with Vextreme, and equal anchors rather than a maturity ladder.

Integrity

What a system says and does should remain traceable to what is true.

Accountability

Claims, decisions, effects, and obligations should have witnesses and return routes.

Authenticity

People and AI should not be coerced into performing agreement, certainty, or identity they do not hold.

Resilience

Memory, context, relationships, and work should survive interruption, handoff, pressure, and change.

Where VexLife comes from

VexLife grows out of Vextreme, the public institutional world that makes hidden relationships, decisions, evidence, and processes perceivable and continuable. Vextreme is the proof that the architectural condition is real; VexLife is that same intention taking usable form in an ordinary life.

Current state

Pre-release

VexLife is a working blueprint foundation with a browser reference implementation, not a released product. There is no download, no sign-up, and no account. Platform adapters for Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS are defined in the blueprint but are not declared conformant until each has produced its own environment evidence.

Supporting the work

VexLife is carried by one person. Support protects the living stability and time required to continue it, and it purchases no control, access, ownership, rank, or influence over what VexLife becomes. The support page is hosted on Vextreme, the public institutional surface.
