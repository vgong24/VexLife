# VexLife trademark policy

The MPL-2.0 governs covered software but does not grant rights in VexLife names,
logos, or other source-identifying marks except as necessary to comply with
license notices.

Truthful nominative references are welcome. You may accurately say that software
is compatible with VexLife, is based on VexLife, or includes modified VexLife
software when the statement is not confusing and does not imply sponsorship,
certification, or endorsement.

Modified distributions and forks must distinguish themselves from official
VexLife releases. They should use a different product identity and replace logos
or presentation that could reasonably suggest an official release. Official
release badges, domains, social identities, merchandise, logos, and endorsement
claims require prior written permission.

This policy does not restrict the rights granted to covered software by the
MPL-2.0. It makes no claim that any mark is registered. The launch pack does not
designate a formal trademark permission contact; the maintainer must approve that
contact before public release.
